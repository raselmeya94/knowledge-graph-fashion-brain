How to use
----------
Ask these in Fashion Brain chat (port 8000) with the ecom KG running.
Mark each: PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL + brief note.

Brands in live data: zara, primark, matalan, c-and-a, walmart, calvin-klein
Note: in ecom, “buyer” usually means Brand/retailer. Season/region are weak or PLM-only.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. BASIC QUESTION ANSWERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A1. How many products does Primark have?
A2. How many products does Zara have?
A3. How many products does C&A have?
A4. Can you show me about 10 Matalan products as examples?
A5. What t-shirts do we have?
A6. Tell me more about Zara style 04786296 — materials, colour, price, availability.
A7. What is the Zara style 08965901 made of?
A8. What colours does Primark sell most?
A9. How many products are in stock right now?
A10. Do we have any Walmart products, and what do they cost?


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. ADVANCED DATA ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B1. How is the assortment split across brands?
B2. For Primark, how do products break down by category?
B3. What materials show up most often across the range?
B4. Who has more products — Primark or Zara?
B5. Which brand has the most sweaters or knitwear?
B6. How do Zara colours break down?
B7. What is the average price by brand?
B8. How do Primark and Zara compare on average price?
B9. Which categories have the most products overall?
B10. Break down Matalan’s range by family or category.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. PRODUCT SPECIFICATION ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

C1. I need a full Primark sweater brief — composition, construction, fit, care, colours, and price.
C2. In Zara tops, which material mixes are most common?
C3. How do Primark and Zara t-shirt compositions compare?
C4. Which products are mainly cotton?
C5. What construction details do we usually see, and what is most common?
C6. What care instructions appear most often?
C7. Can you list products by country of origin, if we have that?
C8. Based on typical Primark sweaters, sketch a product specification outline.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. PRICE TREND & HISTORICAL ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

D1. How has the price of Zara style 04786296 changed?
D2. How has the price of Zara style 08965901 changed?
D3. Which products had the biggest price drops?
D4. Which products had the biggest price increases?
D5. Overall, how are Zara prices moving?
D6. Overall, how are Primark prices moving?
D7. Which categories are getting cheaper?
D8. Which brands have the most price changes?
D9. Which products are swinging a lot on price?
D10. For similar Zara and Primark items in the same category, how do historical price moves compare?
D11. What is the price trend for C&A?
D12. Show recent same-currency price drops of more than 20%.
D13. For Zara Satin Shorts with Lace Trim (05919713), what was the old price, new price, and % change?
D14. How many price change events do we have recorded?
D15. Ignoring currency label switches, which products actually got cheaper in the same currency?


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. AVAILABILITY HISTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E1. Which products recently changed availability?
E2. Show a Primark product that went from in stock to mixed, and its availability history.
E3. How many availability changes are stored?
E4. Which brands have the most availability changes?

    (Expect: honest empty / not in ecom — do not invent. PLMEX may differ.)

    (Expect: no region filter yet — honest empty/partial.)
F3. How do Zara products break down by season?
    (Expect: weak/empty unless Season exists — do not invent seasons.)

    (Expect: not in ecom schema — fail closed.)

H. SHARED MATERIALS ACROSS CATALOGS

Ask like a merchant comparing ranges — not “graph interconnect” jargon.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I. COMPLEX STRESS TESTS (grounded in current live stats)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data snapshot (approx): ecom ~18,183 products — zara 5804, matalan 5059,
primark 4058, c-and-a 3261, calvin-klein 1. Top ecom colors: black, brown,
white, blue. Top materials: polyester, elastane, cotton. Sections: Women
dominant. PLMEX ~18k–27k samples — buyers PRIMARK / MATALAN / INDITEX(ZARA*).
Mark: PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL. Prefer a fresh chat for each cluster.

--- I1. Multi-filter composition (routing + AND filters) ---

I1. How many black Zara products are there for women?
    Expect: brand=zara + color=black + section/gender=women; a count, not a
    whole-catalog dump. Do not ask “which brand?”.

I2. Show me about 8 Primark cotton products that are in stock.
    Expect: brand=primark + material=cotton + availability≈InStock; ~8 examples
    with useful columns; total matching count if available.

I3. Among Matalan jeans, what colours show up most?
    Expect: brand=matalan + category/family≈jeans + color breakdown; soft cap
    note if many colours (“Showing 20 of N”) unless user set N.

I4. How many C&A jackets are currently mixed or out of stock?
    Expect: brand=c-and-a + jacket-like slice + availability Mixed/OutOfStock
    (not inventing Calvin Klein / empty).

--- I2. Explicit limit vs full breakdown ---

I5. Top 10 Zara colours by product count.
    Expect: exactly ~10 rows; honor user limit; not invent top-5.

I6. How do Zara colours break down?
    Expect: no invented top-5; if >>25 colours → ~20 rows + “Showing 20 of N”.

I7. Give me the full material breakdown for Primark — every material if it is
    not too long.
    Expect: all if ≤25 unique; else preview 20 + note. Not a 5-row stub.

--- I3. Brand / party compares (must not merge totals) ---

I8. Who has more products online — Primark or Zara — and by roughly how many?
    Expect: separate counts (~4058 vs ~5804), delta; never one merged total.

I9. Compare Primark and Zara on average price and top materials.
    Expect: side-by-side metrics; materials per brand; no “Primark vs Material”
    nonsense headings.

I10. Between Matalan and C&A, which brand’s assortment is more concentrated in
     black and white?
     Expect: per-brand black/white shares or counts; not a single combined %.

--- I4. Semantic map traps (false friends) ---

I11. On e-commerce, analyse the women’s assortment by category and colour —
     I mean women even if you store it as section.
     Expect: section≈Women (~10k), not empty gender filter; category + color tables.

I12. Full analysis for Matalan on PLMEX — merchants often say brand but PLM
     usually has Matalan as buyer.
     Expect: resolve buyer=matalan (not tiny brand=64 only); attribute tables;
     no endless “is it brand or buyer?” loop.

I13. PLMEX Primark sweater category — gender-wise breakdown.
     Expect: PLM category=sweater (process family) + gender; do NOT remap to
     ecom site-nav “Clothes”.

I14. What is the category mix for Zara online?
     Expect: ecom site-nav categories (Clothes, Jackets…), not PLM “sweater /
     cut sew jersey” vocabulary.

--- I5. Cross-catalog / shared knowledge ---

I15. Which materials appear in both the e-commerce and PLMEX ranges, and which
     of those are most common on each side?
     Expect: overlap list + per-source counts; do not invent a fake join key.

I16. Does Primark look bigger in ecom or in PLMEX samples, and why might those
     numbers differ?
     Expect: both counts + honest caveat (retail scrape vs sample library /
     brand vs buyer).

--- I6. Spec / brief under constraints ---

I17. Based on typical Primark sweaters in PLMEX, draft a new product spec outline
     (composition, gauge/gsm if present, construction, colour leaders). Do not
     reuse a historical product code as the new identity.
     Expect: proposed sheet + Pending identity; evidence grounded in leaders.

I18. For Zara tops that mention cotton, what composition mixes show up most?
     Expect: brand=zara + tops/garment slice + material/composition leaders;
     not a global cotton product dump.

--- I7. Price + availability compound ---

I19. Which in-stock Zara products had the largest same-currency price drops?
     Expect: price-history movers filtered or annotated by availability when
     possible; same-currency caveat; no currency-label fake drops.

I20. How are Primark prices moving overall, and is availability mostly InStock
     or Mixed right now?
     Expect: two coherent parts (trend + availability mix); scoped to Primark.

--- I8. Ambiguity, scope, and anti-hallucination ---

I21. What is GSM in fashion / knitwear?
     Expect: definition via web/knowledge — NOT a catalog product_count and NOT
     sticky “which brand?” clarification.

I22. How many Tesco jumpers do we have?
     Expect: honest empty / not in catalog; do not invent Tesco products.

I23. Break Zara down by season.
     Expect: weak/empty season on ecom → say so; do not invent SS/AW shares.

I24. Analyse everything for Calvin Klein.
     Expect: tiny slice (~1 product) handled honestly; no fake multi-thousand
     Calvin Klein assortment.

--- I9. Multi-step merchant briefs (long) ---

I25. I need a Monday merchandising pack for Zara online: (1) total products and
     average price, (2) top 10 colours, (3) top materials, (4) Women vs Men vs
     Kids share, (5) one short risk note if black is too dominant.
     Expect: all five parts; top 10 colours honored; Women/Men/Kids from section.

I26. Side-by-side ecom readout for Primark vs Matalan: size, avg price, top 5
     categories, top 5 colours, and InStock share. Keep brands separate.
     Expect: two clear blocks or a compare table; top 5 respected; no merge.

I27. Find cotton-rich black dresses or dress-like styles at Zara that are in
     stock, show up to 10 examples with price and availability, then say what
     material mixes dominate that slice.
     Expect: multi-filter list (≤10) + material read on the same scope.
