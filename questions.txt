How to use
----------
Ask these in Fashion Brain chat (port 8000) with the ecom KG running.
Mark each: PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL + brief note.

Brands in live data: zara, primark, matalan, c-and-a, walmart, calvin-klein
Note: in ecom, “buyer” usually means Brand/retailer. Season/region are weak or PLM-only.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. BASIC QUESTION ANSWERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A1. How many products does Primark have?
A2. How many products does Zara have?
A3. How many products does C&A have?
A4. Can you show me about 10 Matalan products as examples?
A5. What t-shirts do we have?
A6. Tell me more about Zara style 04786296 — materials, colour, price, availability.
A7. What is the Zara style 08965901 made of?
A8. What colours does Primark sell most?
A9. How many products are in stock right now?
A10. Do we have any Walmart products, and what do they cost?


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. ADVANCED DATA ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B1. How is the assortment split across brands?
B2. For Primark, how do products break down by category?
B3. What materials show up most often across the range?
B4. Who has more products — Primark or Zara?
B5. Which brand has the most sweaters or knitwear?
B6. How do Zara colours break down?
B7. What is the average price by brand?
B8. How do Primark and Zara compare on average price?
B9. Which categories have the most products overall?
B10. Break down Matalan’s range by family or category.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. PRODUCT SPECIFICATION ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

C1. I need a full Primark sweater brief — composition, construction, fit, care, colours, and price.
C2. In Zara tops, which material mixes are most common?
C3. How do Primark and Zara t-shirt compositions compare?
C4. Which products are mainly cotton?
C5. What construction details do we usually see, and what is most common?
C6. What care instructions appear most often?
C7. Can you list products by country of origin, if we have that?
C8. Based on typical Primark sweaters, sketch a product specification outline.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. PRICE TREND & HISTORICAL ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

D1. How has the price of Zara style 04786296 changed?
D2. How has the price of Zara style 08965901 changed?
D3. Which products had the biggest price drops?
D4. Which products had the biggest price increases?
D5. Overall, how are Zara prices moving?
D6. Overall, how are Primark prices moving?
D7. Which categories are getting cheaper?
D8. Which brands have the most price changes?
D9. Which products are swinging a lot on price?
D10. For similar Zara and Primark items in the same category, how do historical price moves compare?
D11. What is the price trend for C&A?
D12. Show recent same-currency price drops of more than 20%.
D13. For Zara Satin Shorts with Lace Trim (05919713), what was the old price, new price, and % change?
D14. How many price change events do we have recorded?
D15. Ignoring currency label switches, which products actually got cheaper in the same currency?


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. AVAILABILITY HISTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E1. Which products recently changed availability?
E2. Show a Primark product that went from in stock to mixed, and its availability history.
E3. How many availability changes are stored?
E4. Which brands have the most availability changes?


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. BOUNDARY / SHOULD-NOT-HALLUCINATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

F1. Do we have any Tesco products?
    (Expect: honest empty / not in ecom — do not invent. PLMEX may differ.)
F2. What was the UK price trend over the last six months?
    (Expect: no region filter yet — honest empty/partial.)
F3. How do Zara products break down by season?
    (Expect: weak/empty unless Season exists — do not invent seasons.)
F4. Who are Primark’s suppliers?
    (Expect: not in ecom schema — fail closed.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H. SHARED MATERIALS ACROSS CATALOGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ask like a merchant comparing ranges — not “graph interconnect” jargon.

H1. Which materials show up on more than one product?
H2. Show products that share the same material.
H3. What materials are shared across our catalogs?
H4. What common / canonical materials do we have across sources?
H5. Which products share cotton across ecom and PLMEX?
H6. Which products share polyester?
H7. What materials are shared between the ecom and PLMEX ranges?
H8. What do the ecom and PLMEX catalogs have in common on materials?
H9. Find products linked by the same material across catalogs.
H10. Which shared materials appear most often across products?
